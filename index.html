About Us

Rough Rider Racing (RRR) is the University of Ottawa Faculty of Engineering’s Baja SAE team. Baja SAE is an international competition organised by SAE International that challenges students to design and build a single-seat utility terrain vehicle (UTV) built to withstand harsh off-road conditions. There are three North American events annually, each bringing together around 100 student teams.

After a pause during the COVID-19 pandemic, the team has been re-started and now includes about 40 students from mechanical, electrical, civil, software, computer and biomedical mechanical engineering. Our work is organised into five subteams: Admin, Drive Train, Electrical, Suspension and Chassis. Through Baja SAE we gain hands-on experience in design, fabrication, testing and project management that complements what we learn in the classroom.

Sponsorship Opportunities

Rough Rider Racing offers sponsorship opportunities that give partners visibility on campus and access to curated resume booklets, lunch and learns, workshops and recruiting events that connect them directly with engaged students.

Bronze

$250 - $499

  • Small logo on our paddock banner and car
  • Logo listed on our website in the Bronze sponsors section
  • Small-sized logo on our website, posters, banner and team shirts
  • Grouped social media thank-you post with other Bronze sponsors each season

Silver

$500 - $2,499

  • Medium-sized logo on the car or banner
  • Medium-sized logo on our website, posters, banner and team shirts
  • One lunch and learn or technical workshop per year
  • Invitation to join a design review or shop tour with the team
  • Option to send a short company blurb or QR code for our team bulletin board and Teams channels

Gold

$2,500 - $7,499

  • Featured sponsor spotlight on Instagram and LinkedIn each semester, plus logo in our season recap video
  • Featured page in our semesterly resume booklet and early access to curated resumes of active team members
  • Medium-sized logo on the car or banner
  • Medium-sized logo on our website, posters, banner and team shirts
  • Invitation to join a design review or shop tour with the team

Platinum

$7,500+

  • Largest logo on the car or banner in a prime location
  • Largest logo and top placement on our website, posters, banner and team shirts
  • Naming rights to something visible, such as a car nickname, pit canopy or main event
  • Priority access to host lunch and learns, workshops or mini hackathons with our members
  • Featured access to our resume booklet and recruiting opportunities throughout the year

In-kind contributions that directly offset our out-of-pocket costs are given an equivalent dollar value and matched to the Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum tier. Software provided under standard academic or educational licensing programs is recognized separately in our Software Partners category and is not tiered by monetary level.
